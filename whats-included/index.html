Who does what

What we do. What you do.

Your part

  • Add each client with a 10-minute form in your hub
  • Join a 30-minute setup call per client
  • Connect lead sources: paste the website snippet, connect the Facebook Page, add our lead address to Google lead forms, place the new number in ads and on Google
  • Add the email records, or give us access
  • Get your client's SMS registration details to our team: legal name, EIN and a contact person
  • Stay your client's point of contact

Our part

  • Set up each client's account from our standard system, and test it before go-live
  • Buy and set up the local number
  • Enter the SMS registration details, submit them, and fix anything the carriers ask for. Approval is up to the carriers
  • Run the setup call with you
  • Review message edits our checks flag
  • Apply changes to timing, lead sources, contact hours and alert recipients
  • Keep the standard system up to date for every client
  • Answer tickets within 4 business hours
